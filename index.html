How it works

  1. Fingerprint. Your browser calculates the file's SHA-256 fingerprint – a unique 64-character code. Change one letter in the file and the fingerprint is completely different.
  2. Stamp. Only this fingerprint is sent to the free, public OpenTimestamps servers. They collect many fingerprints and write them into one Bitcoin transaction.
  3. Keep the proof. You download a small .ots proof file. Keep it together with your original file.
  4. Bitcoin confirms. After a few hours the proof is in a Bitcoin block. From then on, anyone can check when your file existed – no notary, company or government needed.

🔒 Private by design: your file is never uploaded. Only its fingerprint leaves your computer, and the file cannot be rebuilt from it.

Tip: on a Mac, .ots files may show up as "OpenDocument Template". That is just a name clash with another program – don't open them, simply keep them.
